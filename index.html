Rule of the Game

  • Team Division: The class will be divided into 2 teams (Team A and Team B).

  • Answering: Each chosen number corresponds to a vocabulary challenge. The active team must discuss and provide the correct answer.
  • Scoring & Mystery Rewards:
    • If the team answers correctly, they will unlock a mystery reward.
    • The reward points are completely random, so be careful with your choice.
  • Winning Condition: The team with the highest score at the end of the game wins!
